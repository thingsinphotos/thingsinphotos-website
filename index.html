A little about the idea

Good photos make people look twice.

I'm building ThingsInPhotos around a simple idea: taking thoughtful, characterful photographs of products and objects. Not everything needs to look like a generic catalogue shot.

I enjoy the materials, finishes, shapes and little details that make something worth looking at. The aim is to create images that give your products a bit of personality.

How it works

Based in Sussex, I can photograph products locally or work with items sent to me by post. We can discuss what you need, the look you're after and how the images will be used.

Whether you're an independent brand, a maker or a small business, let's see what we can create.
