What I do

Good products deserve good photos.

Whether you're selling online, launching a new product or building your brand, good photography helps people understand what makes your product worth a closer look.

I create considered product and macro photography, from clean, detailed shots to more creative images that bring out a product's personality. The aim is simple: make people stop scrolling and take notice.

Based in Sussex. Open to projects further afield. Local businesses can arrange a shoot in Sussex. If you're elsewhere in the UK, get in touch about posting your products to me for photography.
